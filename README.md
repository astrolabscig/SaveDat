# SaveDat

SaveDat is an open-source command-line tool, written in Java, that shows which of your files exist in only one place.

It exists because storage devices fail without warning, and most people cannot say what they would lose if one disappeared. SaveDat looks at the folders you point it at, identifies files by their content rather than their names, and reports what has no copy on a different device.

## Status

Early development. It does not do anything useful yet. Right now it only checks whether the paths you give it are directories.

## What it will not do

SaveDat only reads. It never copies, moves, changes or deletes your files, and it does not back anything up.
